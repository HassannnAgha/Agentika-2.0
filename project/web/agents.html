Four agents, one gate

Each agent reads the shared business memory and proposes. None can act. Every proposal goes through the gate, which says how much of you it needs, and why. Try the awkward cases: an upset client, a message that tries to pull another client's details into a reply, a first contact with a stranger.

Operations agentreads correspondence
Replies to recorded clients, flags unhappy or quiet relationships. A sender it does not know becomes an enquiry for the Sales agent, which is not unsolicited contact because they wrote first.
Project Management agentuses recorded skills
Breaks a brief into subtasks and proposes who does each from recorded skills and weekly capacity, citing the team record it relied on.
Marketing agentdrafts, never publishes
Drafts in your recorded voice using only your permitted claims. Publishing is a separate act that two floor rules always hold for you.
Sales agentfinds and approaches
Finds businesses, reads their own websites, scores fit and drafts a first email from recorded facts. First contact is always held for you by floor rule F2.
Open lead generation
Enquiries that arrive in the inbox are handled in the Operations panel and routed to this agent.
