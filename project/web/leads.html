Sales agent: find and approach new clients

The agent finds businesses from public listings, reads each one's own website for a public email and what it can truthfully say about them, scores the fit, and drafts a first email. It cannot send it. First contact with someone who has not written to you is held for your decision by a fixed rule, however good the agent gets.

Public OpenStreetMap listings, one search per click. If the network is down you get clearly labelled sample leads.
Public listings rarely include an email. This reads the site itself (robots.txt respected), takes its public contact email, and notes only what the site actually shows.
